The basics

What is AI?

  • Software that performs tasks that normally need human intelligence.
  • Recognizing images, understanding language, making decisions, generating content.
  • Not magic: it finds patterns in data and uses them to make predictions.
  • Today's AI is narrow: very good at specific tasks, not generally "thinking" like a person.

Reality check

Strengths & limits

Good at

  • Spotting patterns at scale
  • Speed and consistency
  • Drafting, summarizing, translating

Struggles with

  • Being confidently wrong ("hallucinations")
  • True understanding and common sense
  • Anything unlike its training data
Responsibility

Risks & ethics

  • Bias: models can inherit unfairness from their training data.
  • Privacy: what data is collected, and how is it used?
  • Misinformation: realistic fake text, images, and audio.
  • Jobs: tasks change, new roles appear, and people need to adapt.
  • Accountability: humans should stay responsible for important decisions.
Takeaways

What to remember

  • AI learns patterns from data; it isn't magic.
  • It's a powerful tool, best used with human judgment.
  • Always verify important outputs.
  • Stay curious: the field changes fast, so keep learning.
